Geoinformatics engineer from **NUST** (fully funded merit scholarship), based in Islamabad. Since 2023 I have shipped production GIS for water, logistics, health, and the environment, and lately most of my time goes into computer vision on satellite imagery.

I am a **Full-Stack GIS Developer at Horizon Tech Services**. Before that I built apps at **Jugrafiya**, including the Nama Water Portal and 3D survey visualisations for clients in Oman.

## What I work on

- **Web GIS** — interactive maps, dashboards, and 3D city models with React, Node.js, PostGIS, Leaflet, Mapbox, and ArcGIS
- **GeoAI** — custom YOLO detection, Segment Anything segmentation, road extraction, and change detection, written back as map layers
- **Spatial data** — large georeferenced rasters with Python, GeoPandas, Rasterio, and GeoServer
